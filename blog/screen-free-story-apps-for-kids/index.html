Screen-free story time

Screen-Free and Low-Screen Story Options for Kids

Printed books and oral storytelling need no screen. Audio can play with a screen put away; reading text from a phone still uses a screen for the adult. Baboo belongs to the parent-reading option, not a screen-free audio service.

Written by the Baboo Stories team · About 3 min read

Screen-free and low-screen are different

A printed book or a story told from memory can be shared without a device. An audio recording may play after the screen is turned off. When a parent reads text from a phone, the adult is still using a screen, even if the child mainly listens.

“Screen-light” is an informal description of limited screen involvement, not a medical category or an exemption from guidance about media use.

Questions before choosing

  • Do you want to read, or do you want a recorded voice?
  • Can you preview the story and stop it easily?
  • Does playback require a visible screen? Check the actual product.
  • Which content is free, and what requires payment?
  • Would a printed book or a story told from memory fit just as well?

At bedtime

The NHS sleep guidance recommends a predictable wind-down and avoiding screens before bed. Parent involvement does not establish that screen exposure has no effect. A printed book or oral story is available if you want to keep the device away.

Where Baboo fits

Baboo is a story collection for parents to read from their phone to toddlers and young children. It does not provide audio stories or automatic narration. You can adapt the text in your own voice at bedtime or during another quiet reading moment.

Baboo is free to download; an optional monthly subscription unlocks all stories. View the official download options if parent-led phone reading is the format you want.
