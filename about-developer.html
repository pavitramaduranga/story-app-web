The parent behind the app

Meet Pavitra, the parent behind Baboo Stories

I'm Pavitra De Silva, a solo app developer, storyteller, and parent. Baboo Stories began when I started writing the kind of gentle, age-appropriate tales I wanted to share with my own child.

I now build that same thoughtful story experience for families who want bedtime to feel calmer, closer, and easier to enjoy together.

A personal note

Why I created Baboo Stories

Reading with my child is one of the best parts of my day. But finding stories that felt thoughtful, age-appropriate, and easy to return to night after night was harder than I expected.

So I began writing my own: simple stories with warmth, imagination, and something meaningful to talk about afterwards. Those evenings became the starting point for Baboo Stories.

Today, I write and build Baboo Stories with the same question in mind: will this help a parent and child share a good moment together? The app is available on the App Store and Google Play, and I continue improving it through real feedback from families.

Thank you for making Baboo Stories part of your family's reading time.

Pavitra De Silva
Founder of Baboo Stories
